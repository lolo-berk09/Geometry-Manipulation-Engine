# Geometry-Manipulation-Engine
An interactive geometry visualization and exploration tool designed to help students understand the mathematics behind geometric shapes, measurements, and relationships.
---
## Overview
The engine is an interactive, browser-based application that allows its users to build, manipulate, and explore geometric objects on a coordinate plane.

The project was developed with the goal of making geometry easier to understand and more accessible. The math explorer panel within the engine connects the visual shapes with the calculations and formulas behind them.
---
## Features
Creation
- create and select points on a coordinate plane
- create lines, circles, triangles, rectangles, and polygons
- use adaptive grid snapping for more precise placement
- zoom in and zoom out controls inside the coordinate window

Mathematical Measurements
- calculate distance between two points
- determine line slopes
- identify intersections between lines
- calculate angles and interior angle sums
- determine the area and perimeter of polygons
- calculate circle area and circumference

Visualization
- display geometric intersection and angle measurements
- visualize interior angles of the shapes

Math Explorer
Depending on the selected object,
users can explore:
- mathematical formulas and their applications
- step-by-step calculations
- coordinate substitutions
- geometric properties of shapes
