Coordinate Geometry

d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)

Purpose: Calculates the distance between two points on a coordinate plane.

Variables: (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂) represent the coordinates of the two points.

When to use it: When finding the length of a line segment between two known points.

M = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)

Purpose: Finds the exact midpoint between two points.

Variables: The two coordinate pairs represent the endpoints of a line segment.

When to use it: When finding the point halfway between two endpoints.

m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)

Purpose: Measures the steepness and direction of a line.

Variables: The coordinates represent two points on the line.

When to use it: When determining whether a line rises, falls, or is horizontal. Vertical lines have undefined slope.

Triangles

A = ½bh

Purpose: Calculates the area enclosed by a triangle.

Variables: b = base length; h = perpendicular height.

When to use it: When the base and corresponding height are known.

A = √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c))

Purpose: Calculates a triangle's area using only its three side lengths.

Variables: a, b, and c are side lengths; s = (a + b + c)/2 is the semiperimeter.

When to use it: When all three sides are known but the height is not.

A + B + C = 180°

Purpose: Describes the relationship between a triangle's interior angles.

When to use it: When finding an unknown interior angle.

Polygons

P = s₁ + s₂ + ... + sₙ

Purpose: Calculates the total distance around a polygon.

Variables: Each s represents the length of one side.

When to use it: When finding the total boundary length of a polygon.

S = (n − 2) × 180°

Purpose: Calculates the sum of all interior angles in a polygon.

Variables: n = number of sides.

When to use it: When determining the total interior angle measure of a polygon.

A = ½ |Σ(xᵢyᵢ₊₁ − yᵢxᵢ₊₁)|

Purpose: Calculates the area of a polygon from its vertex coordinates.

Variables: (xᵢ, yᵢ) represent consecutive vertices, with the final vertex connected back to the first.

When to use it: When calculating the area of a polygon on a coordinate plane.

Circles

A = πr²

Purpose: Calculates the area enclosed by a circle.

Variables: r = radius.

When to use it: When finding the amount of two-dimensional space inside a circle.

C = 2πr = πd

Purpose: Calculates the distance around a circle.

Variables: r = radius; d = diameter.

When to use it: When finding the perimeter of a circular shape.

General Geometry

a² + b² = c²

Purpose: Describes the relationship between the sides of a right triangle.

Variables: a and b are the legs; c is the hypotenuse.

When to use it: When finding a missing side length in a right triangle.
